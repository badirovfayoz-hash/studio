Privacy Policy

Faymag Studio · last updated 2026-11-23

Faymag Studio makes short AI-generated video ads. This page explains what data our website, our Telegram order bot and our own publishing tool “Faymag Joylash” handle. These tools have no servers of their own: they run on the studio owner's computer. We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics, ads, trackers or forms. GitHub may log basic technical data (such as your IP address) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Telegram order bot

If you write to our Telegram bot (linked on our home page), Telegram gives us your Telegram user ID, first name, username and language, and the answers and messages you send. We use them only to reply to you, to make your free sample and to deliver your order. The text you write about your business and your questions is processed by an AI service (DeepSeek) to draft ideas and replies; answers to your own questions are checked by the studio owner before they are sent. Samples are made from that idea with AI video tools. Your data is stored on the studio owner's computer and is not shared with anyone else. Send /delete to the bot at any time to delete your data; for a paid order we keep only the order record, without your Telegram details.

Faymag Joylash — our own YouTube channels

“Faymag Joylash” is a private desktop tool that the studio owner uses to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account. It uses the YouTube API Services: by using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When the owner connects a channel, Google asks for permission to: upload videos (youtube.upload); see the channel, its videos and their statistics, read-only (youtube.readonly); and, for our content-planning assistant, see the channel's YouTube Analytics reports, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly). We use this data only to upload our videos, to show which channel is connected and to plan our own content. The access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted by the operating system (Windows DPAPI). Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google itself, and nothing is shared, sold or used for advertising.

Faymag Joylash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services).

Facebook and Instagram

The same tool publishes our own videos and captions to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts through Meta's official Graph API, and reads the statistics of our own posts to plan content. Its tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted the same way.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. For questions or deletion requests, write to our Telegram bot (linked on our home page).
